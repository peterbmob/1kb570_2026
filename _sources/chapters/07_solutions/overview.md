# Part VIII: Exercise Solutions

Worked solutions to every exercise in Parts I–VI, organized to match the
book's own part structure — one page per part.

Each exercise gets a short **hint** first, visible immediately, meant to
point you in the right direction without giving the answer away. The full
worked solution — code, results, and explanation — sits inside a collapsed
**"Show full solution"** panel underneath: try the exercise yourself first,
then click to check your approach or see one way to finish it.

There is rarely only one correct way to solve an exercise, especially the
open-ended "design your own study" ones — treat these as *one* worked
solution, not *the* solution, and expect your own approach to differ in
the details while landing on the same conclusions.
